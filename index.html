Print your QR marker

Print, cut, and tape this QR securely to the treadmill belt. Aim the camera so the marker area is clearly visible.

Target data: R

Tip: Use bright lighting. Place the camera steady and angled so the belt surface fills most of the frame.

No calibration required. The app counts one rotation each time the QR marker appears and then leaves the view.
